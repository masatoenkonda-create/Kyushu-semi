# Evidence register — worldwide chip journey

Checked: 20 October 2026  
Purpose: support the high-level route in [GUIDELINES.md](GUIDELINES.md). These sources were opened and read for the claims described below. This is an initial evidence register, not a completed manufacturing specification.

## Read this before using the route

The selected country sequence is a **game scenario** assembled from evidenced industrial activities. None of the records below proves that these companies form one connected supplier chain, or that this route manufactures a particular iPhone part.

Use sources at the scope they support. A company product page establishes a capability; a site page establishes a location. Neither alone proves a shipment, a customer contract, or that every process shown happens at that site.

Do not mark a claim “fact” merely because it sounds familiar. Store the supported statement and its limits. Parameters, travel times, batch sizes and gameplay outcomes remain game assumptions unless separately evidenced.

**Publisher/date:** OECD, June 2025, *Mapping the semiconductor value chain: Working towards identifying dependencies and vulnerabilities*.  
**Source:** [Official report](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/06/mapping-the-semiconductor-value-chain_5ba52971/4154cdbf-en.pdf).

**Limit:** sector-level mapping, with historical data; not a specific product itinerary, factory assignment or company contract. No market-share statistics are imported into this prototype.

## R03 — Quartz and silicon metal; Norway examples

## R08 — Lithography, patterning and equipment geography
